Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Short version: this site has no advertising, no social pixels and no third-party analytics. It collects statistics about how the portfolio is used only if you accept them, and it stores the message you send through the contact form so I can reply. You can ask me to delete anything about you, at any time, by emailing alirezashabanzadeh01@gmail.com.

1. Who is responsible for your data

The controller for this website is Alireza Shabanzadeh, a freelance motion graphics designer based in Rasht, Guilan, Iran. Contact: alirezashabanzadeh01@gmail.com.

2. What is collected, and why

a) Contact form (you send it, I answer it)

  • What: the name, email address, optional project type and message you type.
  • Why: to answer your enquiry and, if we work together, to prepare your project.
  • Where it lives: in the website's own database on the hosting server and in the site owner's private admin panel. It is not used for newsletters, profiling or advertising.
  • How long: until you ask me to delete it, or up to 24 months after our last exchange. The panel keeps a maximum of 500 messages.
  • Legal basis: your request for a quote or information (pre-contractual steps) and my legitimate interest in answering professional enquiries.

b) Visitor statistics (only with your consent)

If you press “Accept statistics” in the banner, the site records:

  • the page you viewed (path and anchor, e.g. /#work),
  • the type of action: page view, “still on page” heartbeat every 30 seconds while the tab is visible, project opened, résumé downloaded,
  • a random pseudonymous visitor id (stored in your browser) and a random session id,
  • a coarse time zone string such as Asia/Tehran (taken from your browser — no GPS, no IP-to-location lookup),
  • a one-way hash of your IP address (SHA-256 with a server-side secret), used only to count unique visitors. The raw IP address is never stored in the statistics file.
  • Why: to understand which work and which pages get attention, so the portfolio can be improved. This is first-party analytics — no Google Analytics, no Meta pixel, no heatmap vendor.
  • How long: statistics are pruned automatically after 30 days.
  • Legal basis: your consent. You can withdraw it at any time with the “Cookie settings” link in the footer.

If you do not accept, no identifier is created and no statistics request is sent. The site honours Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track signals automatically.

c) Likes (a site feature, not tracking)

If you use the “like” button on a project, a pseudonymous id from your browser is stored next to that project so the count stays accurate and you can undo your like. Clearing your browser storage removes it.

d) Server and security logs

Like every website, the hosting server processes technical data (IP address, browser type, time of the request) to deliver pages and to protect against abuse. This is handled by the hosting provider under its own policy and is kept for a short period for security purposes.

e) Video embeds (YouTube / Vimeo)

Project videos are embedded through youtube-nocookie.com or player.vimeo.com. Those players are not loaded when the page opens: either you have accepted “media”, or you click a button that says the video will be loaded, which sends your IP address and browser information to YouTube/Google or Vimeo. Their policies apply from that moment: Google privacy policy, Vimeo privacy policy.

3. What this site does NOT do

  • No advertising cookies, no ad networks, no retargeting.
  • No selling, renting or sharing of your data with data brokers.
  • No cross-site tracking, no fingerprinting, no automatic profiling.
  • No newsletter or marketing email: your address is used only to reply to you.
  • No fonts, scripts or styles loaded from third-party CDNs — they are served from this domain.

4. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU GDPR, UK GDPR, or Iran's personal data regulations), you can ask to:

  • see a copy of the data held about you,
  • correct it,
  • delete it (“right to be forgotten”),
  • restrict or object to its processing,
  • receive it in a portable format,
  • withdraw consent for statistics at any time.

Email alirezashabanzadeh01@gmail.com with the subject “data request”. I answer within 30 days. If you believe your data has been handled incorrectly you also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

5. Security

The site runs over HTTPS, the admin panel requires a password login that sets an HttpOnly session cookie, and the content folders are blocked from direct web access. No method of transmission over the internet is perfectly secure, but the amount of personal data involved here is deliberately kept to a minimum.

6. Changes to this policy

If the way data is handled changes, this page is updated and the date at the top changes. Material changes will not be applied to already-collected data without a new consent where one is required.
