Cookie Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

Short version: this site sets no cookies for visitors at all. It uses your browser's local storage for a few functional things (your theme choice, your cookie choice) and, only if you accept statistics, for a random visitor id. There is no advertising or cross-site tracking.

1. Cookies vs. local storage

Cookies are small files a browser sends to a server on every request. Local storage keeps values in the browser only — they are never sent automatically. Both are treated the same way by privacy rules, so both are listed below.

2. What is stored, and for how long

3. Third parties

  • YouTube / Google and Vimeo — only if you accept “media” or deliberately click to load a video. Their players may set their own cookies; that is outside my control. See Google's cookie notice and Vimeo's cookie policy.
  • No other third parties. Fonts, JavaScript and images are all served from this domain. There is no Google Analytics, no advertising network, no social media pixel and no embedded newsletter or chat widget.

4. How to change or withdraw your choice

  • Use the button — it reopens the banner on any page.
  • Choosing “Essential only” stops statistics immediately and deletes the visitor id from your browser.
  • You can also clear storage for this site in your browser settings at any time; nothing breaks, you will simply be asked about cookies again.
  • Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track signals are honoured automatically: with those on, statistics stay off without showing a banner.

5. More information

What data is collected and how to have it deleted is explained in the Privacy Policy. For any question about cookies, write to alirezashabanzadeh01@gmail.com.
